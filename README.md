# The Orbiting Spoon - A Space Diner & Officer Social Mod
*"Because hyperspace nutrient paste gets old after a few cycles or two."*

> You slide into a cracked vinyl booth beneath a flickering neon sign. The vents hum with recycled air, cutting through the faint smell of engine grease and sizzling biomass.
> Across the table, your second-in-command rubs their temples, staring down at a steaming bowl of spacer's stew.
> 
> *"What'll it be, Captain?"*

**The Orbiting Spoon** is a lightweight, cozy roleplay and campaign layer mod for Starsector that adds interactive portside diners and automated culinary fabricators to markets across the Persean Sector.

Instead of treating spaceports as just refueling menus, take a breather between bounties and hyperspace storms to grab a hot regional meal, break bread with your bridge officers, and check in on fleet morale.

---

## Key Features

* **Portside Galley & Graphical Food Menu:**
  * Visit *The Orbiting Spoon* at developed spaceports (Market Size 4+) to browse regional dishes via a custom visual automat UI.
  * Pay standardized commissary tariffs (flat 200 credits) to feed your crew and seated officers without penalizing large fleets.

* **8 Faction-Specific Diners & Regional Cuisines:**
  * Menus, visuals, and atmospheric descriptions dynamically adapt to who controls the market:
    * **Sindrian Diktat:** Butter-poached Volturnian lobster tail and fiery pepper skewers beneath crimson banners of the Supreme Executor.
    * **The Hegemony:** Commissary iron stew, dense armor-grade hardtack, and chicory coffee under disciplined naval inspection.
    * **Tri-Tachyon:** Laser-seared synthetic prime steak, nutrient gel matrices, and nootropic soda in a clinical corporate automat.
    * **Luddic Church:** Pilgrim's Hearth barley and fowl pie, hand-churned butter, stone-ground sourdough, and spiced Gilead cider.
    * **Luddic Path:** Ascetic turnip mash, unspiced mutton, and bitter penance broth in a chip-hammered bunker.
    * **Pirates & Fringe Outposts:** Charred scraper ribs over open manifolds, peppery fungal goulash, and caustic moonshine.
    * **Persean League:** Rosemary waterfowl skewers, herbed flatbread, saffron rice, and chilled mountain citrus liqueur.
    * **Independent & Frontier Ports:** Loaded spacer's root stew, golden waffles, and scalded black coffee in classic vinyl booths.

* **Wardroom Command Staff & Officer Camaraderie:**
  * Officers accompany you to the diner, responding dynamically based on their **personality** (*Timid, Cautious, Steady, Aggressive, Reckless*) and culinary tastes.
  * Sponsoring meals awards Officer Experience and Fleet Bonus XP.
  * Build long-term **Camaraderie** to unlock unique personality-based campaign doctrines at *Loyal Confidant* status (75%+).

* **Wardroom Tactical Debriefs (Skill Retraining):**
  * Discuss doctrine adjustments over coffee to retire and retrain officer skills (strictly balanced with 1 Story Point and 100,000 credits).

* **The Spoon Directory & Spacer's Field Guide (Intel Tab):**
  * Integrated Fleet Log directory featuring a 3-tab sub-window:
    1. **Sector Registry Table:** Real-time proximity-sorted directory with map centering and tracking to the nearest operational diner.
    2. **Sectors Unknown (Field Notes):** Multi-paragraph culinary dispatches, spoon ratings, and cynical rules of thumb penned by discharged Hegemony auxiliary cook Anton 'Bones' Burndain (with modal dialogue popups).
    3. **Naval Regulations & Morale Dashboard:** Live status tracking for Shore Leave buffs, officer camaraderie levels, and commissary policies.

* **Fleet Morale & Shore Leave:**
  * Enjoying a meal grants a 14-day (*21-day for Independent diner sets*) **Shore Leave** buff:
    * **Perks:** `-5%` Monthly Supply Upkeep, `+5%` Max Combat Readiness (CR), and `+10%` CR Recovery rate.
    * **Downsides:** `+10%` Fleet Sensor Profile (lax signature discipline) and `-5%` Fleet Acceleration (post-meal sluggishness).

* **Cargo Banquets & Humanitarian Aid:**
  * Host private Volturnian Lobster or Pre-Collapse Luxury banquets using cargo commodities for massive camaraderie gains.
  * Donate bulk Food to famine-stricken stations to earn market reputation and brew communal stew for the dockworkers.

* **Emergency Logistics Relief:**
  * Stranded captains with depleted fuel or supplies trigger ancient Domain Relief Protocol 44-B for an emergency rationing package.

## Compatibility & Requirements

* **Game Version:** Starsector 0.98a (Java 17 LTS bytecode)
* **Dependencies:** None (Pure vanilla compatible, no external libraries required)
* **Save Compatibility:** 100% savegame compatible (Safe to install, update, or uninstall mid-campaign without breaking existing saves)
* **Faction Scope:** Spawns on all size 4+ vanilla faction spaceports. Modded factions are safely excluded to preserve intended lore and menu accuracy.
