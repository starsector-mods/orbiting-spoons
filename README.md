# The Orbiting Spoon - A Space Diner & Officer Social Mod
"Because hyperspace nutrient paste gets old after a few cycles or two."

> You slide into a cracked vinyl booth beneath a flickering neon sign. The vents hum with recycled air, cutting through the faint smell of engine grease and sizzling biomass.
> Across the table, your second-in-command rubs their temples, staring down at a lukewarm bowl of spacer's stew.
> 
> "What'll it be, Captain?"

**The Orbiting Spoon** is a lightweight, text-based roleplay and campaign layer mod that adds interactive space diners to markets and spaceports across the Persean Sector.

Instead of treating spaceports as just refueling menus, take a breather between bounties and hyperspace storms to grab a hot meal and check in on your fleet.

---

## Features

* **Sit Down for a Meal:** Visit *The Orbiting Spoon* at any civilized spaceport to order regional food and drinks.
* **Faction-Specific Diners & Menus:** Menus and atmospheric descriptions dynamically adapt to who controls the market:
  * **Sindrian Diktat:** Butter-poached Volturnian lobster tail and spiced fuel-skewers beneath crimson banners of the Supreme Executor.
  * **The Hegemony:** Commissary iron stew, thermal rations, and bitter navy chicory coffee under cold fluorescent lights.
  * **Tri-Tachyon:** Prime vat-grown synth-steak, nutrient gel matrices, and "Phase-Shift" nootropics in a glowing automat.
  * **Luddic Church:** Pilgrim's Hearth harvest pie, fresh sourdough bread with salted butter, and spiced Gilead cider.
  * **Luddic Path:** Austere root mash with salt-mutton, bitter turnip broth, and herbal tea under flickering bare bulbs.
  * **Pirates & Fringe Stations:** Glowing bioluminescent fungal goulash, charred ribs, and "Reactor Leak" moonshine in a grimy dive.
  * **Persean League:** Archon's mezze skewer platter, saffron rice with crispy duck, and spiced citrus aperitifs.
  * **Frontier & Independent Ports:** Retro vinyl booths serving loaded spacer's stew, double waffles, and black "Engine-Joe" coffee.
* **Zero Bloat & Maximum Compatibility:** Purely rule-driven dialogs using transient memory. Safe to add or remove mid-campaign on existing savegames.

## Planned for Future Updates

* Subtle, non-intrusive short-term buffs (e.g., small sensor range boost from strong coffee, minor CR recovery from hearty meals).
* Faction-specific crew rumors and salvage hints overheard in diner booths.
* Expanded unique dialogue for modded/special story characters.
* LunaLib support.
* **Officer & Crew Bonding:**
  * Sitting down brings one of your assigned fleet officers along with their portrait into the dialog.
  * Strike up conversations about fleet life, the food, or past combat engagements.
  * Officer responses branch dynamically based on their **personality** (*Timid, Cautious, Steady, Aggressive, Reckless*).
  * No officers hired? Share a quiet booth with overworked deckhands and mechanics who share spacefarer rumors.

## Compatibility & Requirements
* **Game Version:** Starsector 0.98a (compatible with 0.97a)
* **Dependencies:** None
* **Save Compatibility:** Fully savegame compatible (100% safe to install or uninstall anytime mid-save).
